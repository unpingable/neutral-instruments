# Public instrument pages

Order the front door around the visitor:

1. the useful reading or action;
2. what the visitor can do;
3. what the machine currently says;
4. why the reading can be trusted or doubted;
5. methodology, receipts, and raw data.

Every instrument links plainly to About, Source on GitHub, Methodology, Privacy,
and contact/reporting. About pages answer: what this is; why it exists; what it
looks at; what it does not claim; how to verify it; and who runs it.

Public identity copy should read: “Operated by The Neutral Ambassador
(@neutral.zone). Source code and project history are published by James Beck on
GitHub.” Legal, privacy, and security accountability may name James Beck first.
