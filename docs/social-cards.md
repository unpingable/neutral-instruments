# ATProto Observatory social cards

Cards are static editorial instrument covers, not miniature dashboards. The
project name and plain-language reading stay primary; `ATProto Observatory` is
a small family eyebrow and `neutral.zone` is the custody mark.

The shared contract is a 1200×630 canvas, 66px safe margin, dark slate/bone
base, Source Serif display face, IBM Plex supporting face, and IBM Plex Mono
custody line. Each product owns one accent and one visual grammar. Critical
text must remain legible when the card is reduced to 600×315 and 360×189.

Generated artwork is an input, not a renderer. Retain its prompt, generator
identity, source bytes and digest as provenance. Put all text in a local HTML
overlay, render it with a pinned browser, and record the final PNG digest.
Never put live values, account identifiers, fake controls, or unverifiable
claims into a static card.

The template under `templates/` is deliberately non-executable. Products copy
and specialize it locally, keeping their complete render chain and final asset
inside the product repository. Nothing here creates a runtime dependency.
